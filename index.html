Who talks to whom?

Particles in a jet as a graph: message passing, attention, and local vs global connectivity.

Tap a particle to make it the receiving node i.

Neighbours per particle, k
Message-passing layers, L = 0

One layer: every particle collects messages from its k nearest neighbours in (η, φ), then updates itself:

hi′ = meanj ∈ kNN(i) MLP(hi, hj − hi)

Information flows from each particle's k nearest neighbours into it. After L layers, particle i has received information from its L-hop neighbourhood (coloured; darker = fewer hops). With small k the receptive field stops growing: collinear fragments only talk to each other, and the other prong never arrives, however many layers you add.

Tap a particle (or a heatmap row) to choose the query. Line width ∝ weight relative to uniform 1/N.
